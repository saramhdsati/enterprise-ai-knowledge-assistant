# NovaBridge Technologies Inc. — Departments

**Last revised:** January 5, 2026
**Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Overview

NovaBridge Technologies Inc. is organized into six departments. This document describes each department's mission, typical roles, and how they collaborate with the rest of the Company.

## 2. Engineering

**Mission:** Build and operate the NovaBridge Data Platform reliably and securely.

**Sub-teams:**
- Platform Infrastructure — cloud infrastructure, reliability, and scaling.
- Data Pipelines — the core ingestion and transformation product.
- Security Engineering — application security, works closely with the IT Security team.
- Developer Experience — internal tooling and CI/CD.

**Typical roles:** Software Engineer, Site Reliability Engineer, Security Engineer, Engineering Manager.

## 3. Product

**Mission:** Define what NovaBridge builds next, grounded in customer needs and business goals.

**Sub-teams:**
- Product Management
- Product Design (UX/UI)
- User Research

**Typical roles:** Product Manager, Product Designer, User Researcher.

## 4. Sales

**Mission:** Grow NovaBridge's customer base and revenue.

**Sub-teams:**
- New Business (Account Executives)
- Sales Development (outbound/inbound qualification)
- Sales Engineering (technical pre-sales support)

**Typical roles:** Account Executive, Sales Development Representative, Sales Engineer, Sales Manager.

## 5. Customer Success

**Mission:** Ensure customers achieve value from the NovaBridge Data Platform and renew their contracts.

**Sub-teams:**
- Onboarding & Implementation
- Ongoing Customer Success Management
- Support (technical support tickets)

**Typical roles:** Customer Success Manager, Implementation Specialist, Support Engineer.

## 6. General & Administrative (G&A)

**Mission:** Provide the operational, financial, legal, and people foundations that let every other department run smoothly.

**Sub-teams:**
- People Operations (HR) — hiring, benefits, employee relations, policies such as this handbook.
- Finance & Accounting — payroll, budgeting, vendor payments.
- Legal — contracts, compliance, data protection.
- IT — device provisioning, security tooling, internal systems (see IT Security Policy).

**Typical roles:** People Operations Partner, Recruiter, Financial Analyst, Legal Counsel, IT Support Specialist.

## 7. Executive Team

The Executive Team consists of department leads and the CEO, CTO, and CFO, and meets weekly to align on company-wide priorities, review key metrics, and make cross-department decisions.

## 8. How Departments Collaborate

- **Quarterly Planning:** all departments participate in a company-wide planning cycle each quarter to align goals (OKRs) across teams.
- **All-Hands Meetings:** a company-wide meeting is held monthly to share updates across departments.
- **Internal Procedures:** cross-department workflows (e.g., new hire onboarding, vendor approval) are documented in the Internal Procedures document.
