# NovaBridge Technologies Inc. — Code of Conduct

**Last revised:** January 5, 2026
**Owner:** People Operations & Legal

## 1. Our Commitment

Every employee, contractor, and manager at NovaBridge Technologies Inc. is expected to act with honesty, respect, and professionalism. This Code of Conduct sets the minimum standard of behavior expected across the Company, regardless of role, location, or seniority.

## 2. Respect in the Workplace

- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with courtesy and professionalism, in person and online.
- Harassment, bullying, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind will not be tolerated. This includes conduct based on race, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, or veteran status.
- Disagreements should be handled constructively. Employees are encouraged to raise concerns directly with the person involved when it is safe and appropriate to do so, or to escalate to their manager or People Operations.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

- Employees must disclose any outside employment, financial interest, or personal relationship that could reasonably create a conflict of interest with their role at NovaBridge.
- Employees may not use their position to award business, contracts, or preferential treatment to family members or personal associates without prior disclosure and approval.
- Accepting gifts from vendors or partners beyond nominal value (generally under $75) requires disclosure to a manager.

## 4. Confidential Information

- Employees must protect confidential business information, trade secrets, customer data, and unreleased product plans, consistent with the Employee Handbook, Section 4.7 (Confidentiality).
- Confidential information must not be discussed on public channels, personal social media, or outside the Company without authorization.
- Obligations under this section continue after an employee's employment ends.

## 5. Use of Company Resources

- Company equipment, accounts, and systems are provided for business purposes. Limited, reasonable personal use is permitted as long as it does not interfere with work or violate other Company policies (see IT Security Policy and Device Policy).
- Employees may not use Company resources for personal commercial activity, political campaigning, or any unlawful purpose.

## 6. Compliance with Laws

- Employees must comply with all applicable laws and regulations relevant to their role, including data protection, export control, anti-bribery, and anti-corruption laws.
- Employees must never offer, give, or accept a bribe or improper payment to obtain or retain business.

## 7. Reporting Concerns

- Employees who witness or suspect a violation of this Code of Conduct should report it to their manager, People Operations, or through the confidential ethics reporting channel at ethics@novabridge.example.
- Reports made in good faith are protected. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited and is itself grounds for disciplinary action.

## 8. Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment, consistent with the Employee Handbook, Section 5.8 (Disciplinary Action). Serious violations, including those involving legal violations, may also be reported to appropriate authorities.

## 9. Acknowledgment

All employees are required to read and acknowledge this Code of Conduct upon hire and annually thereafter as part of the Company's compliance training program.
