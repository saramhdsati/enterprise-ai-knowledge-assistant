# NovaBridge Technologies Inc. — Leave Policy

**Last revised:** January 5, 2026
**Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Overview

This policy explains the paid and unpaid leave types available to eligible employees of NovaBridge Technologies Inc., in addition to the Paid Time Off (PTO) categories described in the Employee Handbook, Section 3.5.

## 2. Annual Leave Allowance

| Leave type | Annual allowance |
|---|---|
| Vacation Days | 15 days/year |
| Sick Days | 10 days/year |
| Personal Days | 3 days/year |
| Bereavement Leave | 3 days/year |
| Jury Duty | Up to 5 days (paid, initial period) |
| Voting Leave | 1 day/year |

All figures above are prorated for employees who join partway through the calendar year, based on their start date.

## 3. Requesting Leave

1. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 7 days in advance for non-emergency leave.
2. Sick leave may be reported on the morning of the absence via Slack (#time-off channel) and logged retroactively in the HR portal within 2 business days.
3. Manager approval is required for Vacation Days and Personal Days. Sick Days and Bereavement Leave do not require pre-approval but must be logged.

## 4. Carryover and Forfeiture

- Up to **40 hours** (5 days) of unused Vacation Days may be rolled over into the following calendar year.
- Any Vacation Days beyond the 40-hour rollover cap are forfeited on December 31 unless local law requires otherwise.
- Sick Days, Personal Days, and Bereavement Leave do not roll over and reset each January 1.

## 5. Extended Leave

### 5.1 Medical Leave
Employees with a serious health condition may apply for medical leave under the Family Medical Leave Act (FMLA) where applicable, coordinated with People Operations and, where relevant, short-term disability insurance.

### 5.2 Bereavement Leave Extension
Employees may request up to 2 additional unpaid days beyond the standard 3 paid Bereavement days, subject to manager approval.

### 5.3 Sabbatical
Employees who have completed 5 years of continuous service are eligible to request a 4-week unpaid sabbatical, subject to team capacity planning and approval by their department lead at least 90 days in advance.

## 6. Public Holidays

NovaBridge Technologies Inc. observes the following paid holidays each year:

- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Memorial Day
- Juneteenth
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Christmas Day
- Veterans Day

Employees in locations outside the United States follow the public holiday calendar published for their local entity by People Operations.

## 7. Leave Without Pay

Employees who have exhausted all applicable paid leave may request unpaid leave of absence. Requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis and require approval from the employee's manager and People Operations.
