NOVABRIDGE TECHNOLOGIES INC.
PARENTAL LEAVE POLICY
Last revised: January 5, 2026
Owner: People Operations

1. PURPOSE

NovaBridge Technologies Inc. provides paid parental leave to eligible employees welcoming a new child through birth, adoption, or foster placement, in addition to any minimum leave required under federal, state, or local law.

2. ELIGIBILITY

2.1 All full-time employees who have completed at least 6 months of continuous service are eligible for paid parental leave.

2.2 Employees with less than 6 months of service are eligible for unpaid parental leave in accordance with applicable law.

2.3 The policy applies equally regardless of gender, and to primary and secondary caregivers, adoptive parents, and foster parents.

3. LEAVE DURATION AND PAY

3.1 Birthing Parent: 12 weeks of fully paid leave, which may run concurrently with any applicable short-term disability benefit related to childbirth recovery.

3.2 Non-Birthing Parent / Secondary Caregiver: 6 weeks of fully paid leave.

3.3 Adoption or Foster Placement: 6 weeks of fully paid leave for the primary caregiver, and 2 weeks for the secondary caregiver, beginning on the date of placement.

3.4 Paid parental leave under this policy is separate from, and does not reduce, an employee's standard Vacation Days or Sick Days balance.

4. TIMING AND FLEXIBILITY

4.1 Parental leave may be taken in a single continuous block or split into two blocks within the first 12 months following the birth, adoption, or placement, subject to manager approval for the second block.

4.2 Employees are encouraged to notify their manager and People Operations at least 30 days before the expected leave start date, or as soon as reasonably possible in the case of early birth or unplanned placement.

5. RETURN TO WORK

5.1 Employees returning from parental leave are guaranteed reinstatement to their same or an equivalent role, with the same pay, benefits, and seniority.

5.2 Employees may request a gradual return to work (e.g., reduced hours for up to 2 weeks) by discussing arrangements with their manager and People Operations in advance.

6. BENEFITS CONTINUATION

6.1 Health insurance and other benefits described in the Health Insurance Policy continue uninterrupted during paid parental leave.

6.2 401(k) contributions may continue at the employee's discretion during leave, subject to plan rules.

7. INTERACTION WITH OTHER LEAVE POLICIES

This policy works alongside, and does not replace, the Family Medical Leave Act (FMLA) provisions described in the Employee Handbook, Section 3.7, and the Leave Policy. Where FMLA and this policy both apply, leave will generally run concurrently, not consecutively, unless otherwise required by law.

8. QUESTIONS

For questions about eligibility, pay calculation, or scheduling parental leave, contact People Operations at hr@novabridge.example at least one payroll cycle before your planned leave date.
