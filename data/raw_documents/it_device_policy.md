# NovaBridge Technologies Inc. — Device Policy

**Last revised:** January 5, 2026
**Owner:** IT Security Team

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the use, security, and management of computing devices used to access NovaBridge Technologies Inc. systems and data, whether Company-issued or personally owned.

## 2. Company-Issued Devices

- All full-time employees are issued a standard laptop (macOS or Windows, per role requirements) configured with Company security software prior to first use.
- Company-issued devices remain the property of NovaBridge Technologies Inc. and must be returned upon termination of employment or role change to a non-eligible position.
- Devices must not be modified to disable security agents, bypass administrator controls, or install unauthorized operating systems.

## 3. Required Security Configuration

All Company-issued devices must have, at minimum:

- Full-disk encryption enabled (BitLocker for Windows, FileVault for macOS).
- Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) agent installed and reporting.
- Automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- Operating system and security patches applied within 14 days of release for standard updates, and within 72 hours for critical security patches.

## 4. Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

- Personal mobile devices (phones, tablets) may be used to access Company email and calendar only through the approved Mobile Device Management (MDM) enrollment.
- BYOD is not permitted for accessing source code repositories, production systems, or customer data.
- Employees who enroll a personal device in MDM acknowledge that the Company may remotely wipe Company data (not personal data) from the device if it is lost, stolen, or the employee leaves the Company.

## 5. Removable Media

- Use of USB drives and other removable media to store Company data is discouraged. Where required for a specific business reason, only Company-approved encrypted USB drives may be used, with manager and IT Security approval.

## 6. Lost or Stolen Devices

1. Report a lost or stolen device to the IT Helpdesk and IT Security **immediately**, and no later than 2 hours after discovery.
2. IT Security will initiate a remote lock and wipe of the device where technically possible.
3. Employees must also change passwords for any accounts that may have been accessible from the device.

## 7. Personal Use

Limited personal use of Company-issued devices is permitted (e.g., checking personal email, browsing) as long as it does not interfere with work duties, consume excessive resources, or violate the Code of Conduct or IT Security Policy.

## 8. Device Return

Upon termination of employment, transfer, or role change requiring a different device configuration, employees must return all Company-issued devices and accessories to IT within 5 business days, following the offboarding checklist provided by People Operations.

## 9. Non-Compliance

Devices found to be out of compliance with this policy (missing encryption, outdated patches, disabled security agents) may have network access automatically restricted until remediated.
