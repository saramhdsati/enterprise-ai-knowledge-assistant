NOVABRIDGE TECHNOLOGIES INC.
PASSWORD POLICY
Last revised: January 5, 2026
Owner: IT Security Team

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines minimum requirements for creating, protecting, and changing passwords used to access NovaBridge Technologies Inc. systems, applications, and data.

2. SCOPE

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who access Company systems, including email, VPN, cloud infrastructure, and internal applications.

3. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS

3.1 All passwords must meet the following minimum requirements:
- Minimum length of 14 characters.
- At least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character.
- Must not contain the employee's username, first name, or last name.
- Must not be a password used within the employee's last 10 passwords.

3.2 Passwords must not be reused across multiple Company systems or between Company and personal accounts.

4. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)

4.1 MFA is mandatory for all accounts with access to email, VPN, source code repositories, and cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP, Azure).

4.2 Approved MFA methods include the Company-approved authenticator app or a hardware security key. SMS-based MFA is discouraged and is only permitted as a fallback method.

5. PASSWORD MANAGEMENT

5.1 All employees are provisioned with a license for the Company-approved password manager and are required to use it to generate and store unique passwords for work accounts.

5.2 Passwords must never be written down in plain text, stored in unencrypted files, or shared over email, chat, or messaging apps.

5.3 Passwords must never be shared between employees, including between a manager and direct report, even temporarily.

6. PASSWORD EXPIRATION AND CHANGES

6.1 Passwords for privileged accounts (system administrators, database administrators) must be changed every 90 days.

6.2 Standard user account passwords do not expire on a fixed schedule but must be changed immediately if compromise is suspected.

6.3 Employees must change their password immediately and report to IT Security if they suspect their credentials have been exposed, phished, or compromised.

7. ACCOUNT LOCKOUT

7.1 Accounts will be automatically locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts within a 15-minute window.

7.2 Locked accounts can be unlocked via self-service password reset or by contacting the IT Helpdesk after identity verification.

8. SERVICE AND SYSTEM ACCOUNTS

8.1 Service accounts and system accounts must use passwords or credentials of at least 20 characters, generated randomly, and stored in the Company's secrets management system — never hard-coded in source code or configuration files.

9. ENFORCEMENT

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment, consistent with the Employee Handbook, Section 5.8, and may result in temporary suspension of system access pending investigation.
